Welcome to Hooked & Twisted!

Discover the joy of crocheting with our collection of free patterns, yarn guides, and helpful resources for beginners.

Beginners Start Here!

Learn the basics of crocheting and gain the confidence to start creating your own projects. Explore essential crochet tools, learn how to hold your hook and yarn, practice beginner-friendly stitches, and discover helpful tips that make learning to crochet simple and enjoyable. Whether you've never picked up a crochet hook before or just need a refresher, this section will help you get started.

Yarn Types

Discover the wonderful variety of yarns available and learn how to choose the right one for your next crochet project. Explore different fibers, weights, textures, and colors while learning about the unique qualities of each type of yarn. Find out which yarns are best for beginners, how yarn choices can affect the look and feel of your finished project, and how to select the perfect yarn for your pattern.

Pets

Create cozy, adorable, and practical crochet projects that your furry friends can enjoy. Discover fun ideas for handmade pet sweaters, blankets, toys, accessories, and other creative projects designed with your pets in mind. Learn how to choose comfortable materials and make projects that are both cute and useful for the special animals in your life.
