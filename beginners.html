Beginner's Crochet Supplies

  • Crochet Hooks (5mm, 5.5mm, or 6mm is recommended for beginners)
  • Yarn (4 weight is recommended for beginners)
  • Scissors
  • Tapestry Needle
  • Stitch Markers
  • Measuring Tape

Clover hooks (as seen in the image to the right) are a popular choice for beginners due to their ergonomic design and smooth finish. They come in various sizes, so you can choose the one that best suits your project. For yarn, acrylic or cotton yarns are great for beginners as they are easy to work with and widely available.

When selecting yarn, consider the weight and texture. A medium-weight Acrylic yarn (from brands such as Red Heart, Loops & Threads, and Big Twist) is ideal for beginners as it is easy to handle and creates a nice fabric. Avoid novelty yarns with lots of texture or fuzz, as they can be challenging to work with when you're just starting out.

Hook Types

Metal / Aluminum

Lightweight and durable, making them a popular choice for crocheters. They come in various sizes and are often color-coded for easy identification.

Wood / Bamboo

Warm and lightweight with slightly more friction on the yarn. The added friction can help with grip, especially when working with fiber that is slippery or smooth.

Ergonomic Soft Grip

Feature thick, soft rubber or silicone handles designed to reduce hand cramps and wrist fatigue. Ergonomic hooks are generally preferred, as they alleviate wrist strain.

Plastic

Inexpensive, lightweight, though they can bend or squeak during use. They are often used for larger hooks and are suitable for beginners on a budget.

Beginner's Crochet Video

This video povides a quick and simple overview of the basic crochet stitches. Including the slip knot, chain stitch, single crochet, and double crochet. These stitches are the foundation of many crochet projects, and mastering them will allow you to create a wide variety of items.

There are many online resources available for beginners, including video tutorials, written patterns, and crochet communities. Don't be afraid to ask questions and seek help from more experienced crocheters. With practice and patience, you'll soon be able to create beautiful crochet projects.
