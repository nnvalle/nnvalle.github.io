Learning to understand yarn labels is crucial for selecting the right yarn for your projects. This is an example of a typical yarn label, showing the yarn weight, fiber content, care instructions, and recommended hook size. Also, pay attention to the Lot number when purchasing yarn, skeins from different lots can have slight variance in color, this can affect the final appearance of your project. Use the below chart as a recommendation for different yarn weights and their corresponding projects.
